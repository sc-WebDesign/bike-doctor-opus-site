## Why these four

They map onto the review record rather than onto a generic bike-shop mood. Google's theme tags for
this business are **flat tyre repair (15)**, **punctuality (15)**, **brake repair (6)** and new gear
cables (3), so the set is tyres, brakes, gears and the workshop.

**The lime gear cable in `drivetrain.jpg` is a happy accident worth keeping**: it matches the hi-vis
lime accent this identity already uses, which was chosen from the visual language of cycling safety
gear rather than from bike shops.

The bike-repair genre photograph is a close-up of a chain and a pair of hands, every single time.
Only one of these four is a drivetrain shot, and it earns its place because gear cables are an
evidenced service.

## Selection method

Every file was verified as a real JPEG by its magic bytes (`ffd8ff`) and then **opened and looked at
before use**. Rejected:

- `248731`: a **motorcycle** wheel, not a bicycle. Chunky tyre with DOT markings, fork legs and a
  drum brake, and monochrome besides.

Component brands are visible on the bikes in two of these (bar tape, tyres, a frame decal). They are
incidental to photographs of bicycles being worked on rather than a claimed relationship with any
manufacturer, which is the distinction drawn on the detailing build where a product bottle with a
dominant brand was rejected outright.
